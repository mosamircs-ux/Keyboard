Keynova Keyboard

Privacy Policy • Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Keynova Keyboard (“we”, “our”, or “us”), developed by Mohamed Samir Amin. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle user data and respect your personal privacy when you use the Keynova Keyboard mobile application for Android.

Fundamental Privacy Guarantee: Keynova Keyboard does NOT collect, record, log, or transmit your keystrokes, typed text, passwords, credit card numbers, personal messages, or private clipboard contents. All typing processing happens 100% locally on your device.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike many cloud-connected keyboards, Keynova Keyboard is built with privacy as its foundation:

  • No Keystroke Logging: We do not log or store what you type.
  • No User Accounts: Keynova does not require an account, sign-in, username, or email to function.
  • No Personal Identification: We do not ask for or collect your real name, address, contact list, or photos.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not collect or access your GPS or physical location.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization

Keynova Keyboard displays banner and interstitial advertisements solely inside its app utility and settings activities. No advertisements are ever displayed inside the active keyboard input overlay (IME).

We use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve advertisements:

  • Google Advertising ID: Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID (AAID) to deliver personalized or non-personalized advertisements, prevent fraud, and analyze ad performance.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): In regions covered by the GDPR, UK GDPR, and other privacy frameworks, we present the Google UMP consent dialog. Users have full control over consent choices and can adjust privacy preferences at any time from the Keynova Settings screen.

For more details on how Google processes data, please visit Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions Requested

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used exclusively by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request and load advertisements in the app settings and companion activity screens.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID: Used by Google Play Services to obtain the advertising identifier for ad attribution and policy-compliant advertising.
  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide optional haptic feedback when pressing keys.

5. Data Security

All network communications performed by the Google Mobile Ads SDK are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). No sensitive personal data or typing data is ever generated or transmitted to external servers.

6. Children’s Privacy

Keynova Keyboard is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
